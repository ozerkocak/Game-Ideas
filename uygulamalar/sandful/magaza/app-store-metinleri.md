## Açıklama (Description)

### İngilizce

```
Sandful is a calm, hands-on sand art game.

Hold your finger on the screen and colored sand flows from a little funnel. Move the funnel left and right, follow the faint lines on the glass, and a landscape slowly appears inside the jar: a beach, rolling hills, mountains, a desert, a sunset, a starry night.

The sand behaves like the real thing. It piles up where you wait, spreads thin when you sweep, and slides down the slope when it gets too steep. Every grain whispers as it lands, and your phone ticks softly in your hand.

• 10 handcrafted scenes, from a sunny beach to a moonlit night
• Realistic sand that piles, spreads and slides
• Little details to place: a boat, a swan, the moon
• Soft sand sounds (three to choose from), gentle music and ambient sea, wind and night sounds
• Subtle haptics
• Up to three stars per jar; every finished jar joins your shelf
• No timers, no pressure: play at your own pace
• Plays offline, no account needed
• English and Turkish

Pour, breathe, relax.
```
