Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Sandful (the "Game") is developed by Investa Teknoloji Anonim Şirketi ("we").

Data we collect

The Game does not collect, store or share any personal data. No account is required, and the Game does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (level stars, best results, thumbnails of finished jars) and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the Game deletes this information.

Ads and purchases

This version contains no ads and no in-app purchases. If they are added in the future, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and any required permissions will be requested in the Game.

Children

The Game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
